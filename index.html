StaticVerse ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Imposter Ko? mobile application (the "Application"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download and use Imposter Ko?. We designed Imposter Ko? as a local, pass-the-phone party game. The Application does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal information in order to play. By using the Application, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. About Imposter Ko?

Imposter Ko? is a multiplayer party game designed to be played by people together in the same room using one Android device.

The Application includes features such as:

  • Normal Game Mode — play the classic Imposter game.
  • Custom Mode — create your own secret word and hint.
  • Tournament Mode — play multiple rounds, earn points, and track tournament scores.
  • Country-Specific Word Packs — use word packs designed around different regions, including Nepal, India, and Global content.
  • Pass-the-Phone Gameplay — players use the same device and privately view their roles and words.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

Imposter Ko? does not require you to create an account.

You may voluntarily enter information into the Application while playing, such as:

  • Player names or nicknames
  • Custom secret words
  • Custom hints
  • Tournament player names
  • Game settings and selections

This information is used to provide the game's functionality.

For example, player names are used to identify players during a game and tournament, while custom words and hints are used to create a custom game.

We do not require you to provide your real name or other identifying information.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The Application may store certain game and application settings locally on your Android device.

This may include information such as:

  • Custom game settings

  • Other information required for the Application to function correctly

This local information is used to maintain the Application's functionality between screens or sessions.

The Application uses local device storage rather than requiring an account or our own online database for normal gameplay.
