## 1. About Imposter Ko?

Imposter Ko? is a multiplayer party game designed to be played by people together in the same room using one Android device.

The Application includes features such as:

* **Normal Game Mode** — play the classic Imposter game.
* **Custom Mode** — create your own secret word and hint.
* **Tournament Mode** — play multiple rounds, earn points, and track tournament scores.
* **Country-Specific Word Packs** — use word packs designed around different regions, including Nepal, India, and Global content.
* **Pass-the-Phone Gameplay** — players use the same device and privately view their roles and words.
